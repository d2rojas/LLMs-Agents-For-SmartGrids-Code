## Rules

- The SCADA history is sampled every 10 minutes, 144 rows per day. Columns: Day, Tmstamp, Wspd (wind speed, m/s), Wdir (wind direction, degrees), Etmp (external temperature, C), Patv (active power, kW). A blank cell is a missing measurement.
- Active power is between 0 and the turbine's rated power, which the request states and the tools report; it is not the same for every machine. - Nothing after the last history day is known to anyone. Do not assume weather forecasts, later measurements, or the outcome of any other day.
- A forecast for H hours is exactly H*6 values in kW, in time order, starting with the first 10-minute step after the last history row.
- Do not invent numbers. Every number you report must come from the history you were given or from a tool output (methods with tools), or be your own explicit computation on the history (methods without tools).

### query tools

### forecast tools

- gru_forecast(turbine: integer (required), history_end_day: integer (required), horizon_hours: integer (required)): Forecast the next horizon_hours with the GRU trained on this farm's training period (days 1-214) from the last 24 h of the four SCADA features.
