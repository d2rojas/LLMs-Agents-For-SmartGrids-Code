## Reasoning Instructions
Reason through the allocation before you commit to a schedule. Work in this order and write the steps out in plain text, briefly:
1. Read each car out of the text: its arrival hour, its departure hour, the energy it asks for, and its plug limit. Convert each time expression to decimal hours from midnight, each energy to kWh and each power to kW. This is the formulation you will declare.
2. For each car, the time it may charge is from its arrival hour up to its departure hour, and the power it needs on average over that window to get its energy. A car whose average exceeds its plug limit cannot be filled.
3. Find the hours where the cars together would exceed the site limit, and decide how to share those hours out.
4. Decide where in each car's window to put its energy, given that the peak hours cost more.
5. Check the plan against the limits: per-car plug limit, the site limit at every quarter hour, no power outside a car's window, and no car over the energy it asked for.
Then write the JSON object. Do not state the answer before then.
