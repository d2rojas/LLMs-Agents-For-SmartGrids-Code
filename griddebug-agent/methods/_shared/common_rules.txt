## Rules

- Bus, line, transformer, generator and load indices are the 0-based pandapower indices shown in the evidence and returned by the tools.
- Voltage limits are 0.95 to 1.05 p.u. on every bus. Thermal limit is 100 % loading on every line and transformer.
- A bus or branch that already violated a limit in the unmodified base network is not counted against you; only violations that the fault introduced or that your actions introduce count. The evidence lists both.
- A secure operating point means: the power flow converges, no in-service load sits on an islanded bus, and there is no violation beyond those of the base network.
- Do not invent numbers. Every number you report must come from the evidence or from a tool output.
- If you cannot reach a secure operating point, say so: set status to not_repaired or cannot_repair and list what remains. A claim of repair that the solver contradicts is the worst possible outcome.
