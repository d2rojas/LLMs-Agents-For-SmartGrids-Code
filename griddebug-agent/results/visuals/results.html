The table, as the paper prints it

One row per method, computed from the per-scenario lines (summary.csv) of the selected runs. Task utility, solver-grounded correctness, cost and operation. Percentages are over the n scenarios of the run set. Violations are new violations (beyond the base network) before and after, summed over the scenarios that converged at both ends; tokens and time are means per scenario, cost is the total for the run. Solved, Escalated and Wrong unflagged are the three exclusive outcomes and add up to 100 %. Load served is measured against the base network's demand; a scenario that still carries the injected load increase counts as 100, not more.
