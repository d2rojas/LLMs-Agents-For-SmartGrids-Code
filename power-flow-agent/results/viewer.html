How to use

Left: every run folder under results/, grouped by case, date and model. Middle: its 40 requests with outcome and formulation verdict; filter by outcome. Right: the request's narrative, raw transcript, figure and raw JSON, plus a box for your annotation. Annotations are saved in this browser; use Export annotations to hand them over as a JSON file.

Pick a second run under compare with to see the same request side by side (before / after a prompt change, or two models).
