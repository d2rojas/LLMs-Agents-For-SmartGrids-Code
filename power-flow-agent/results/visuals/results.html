How to read this page

Pick a model and a view. Each tile above is one row of Table 6 for that model: how its 40 requests ended and how many it formulated exactly. Pick a scenario on the left; each column shows that same request under one method as a log of real trace steps (the request, the plan, what the model decided, what the solver returned, the gate, the final answer). Click a line to expand it. The verdict at the bottom of each column is what the table counts. Colored squares in the list summarize the outcome per method: green solved, amber escalated, red wrong and unflagged.
