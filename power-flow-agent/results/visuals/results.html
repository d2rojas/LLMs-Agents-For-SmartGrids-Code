The table, as the paper prints it

One row per method, computed from the per-request lines (summary.csv) of the selected runs. Task utility, solver-grounded correctness, cost and time. Percentages are over the n requests of the run set; Formulation is scored over the requests the method answered (a method that declares it cannot complete a request reports no formulation; that is counted under Escalated). V_MAE (p.u.) and B_mean (MW) are shown as solved / all: the mean over the solved requests, then over every request that reported a state; tokens and time are means per request.
